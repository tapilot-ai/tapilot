Phone does not have Tapilot? Install it first

1. Scan this download QR with your phone camera or browser.

Download Tapilot APK from Feishu · GitHub Releases

Download and install the APK on your Android phone. Allow the browser to install apps when Android asks. Android 11+ / ARM64 required.

2. Open Tapilot settings. Enable Device control in system Accessibility, then enable wireless control.

3. Return here. In Tapilot wireless control, choose Scan computer QR, scan the pairing QR below, then confirm on this computer.

The download QR installs the app; the pairing QR connects it. Keep both devices on reachable Wi-Fi. If pairing expires during installation, generate a new pairing QR.
